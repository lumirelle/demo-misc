The Quiet Power of Restraint

Good typography doesn't announce itself. It sits quietly beneath the words, carrying meaning without demanding attention — and that invisibility is exactly what makes it powerful.

When we choose a typeface for daily reading, we are not choosing a decoration. We are choosing a voice. A serif body text speaks with the cadence of a book; a sans-serif with the clarity of a sign. The best daily typefaces do both: they disappear when you're reading, and reveal their character only when you stop to look.

The three families on this page — serif, sans-serif, and monospace — each have a job. The serif carries long-form prose. The sans-serif handles interface and navigation. The monospace marks data, code, and the small structural details that hold a page together. Switch tabs at the bottom to see how different combinations change the feel entirely.

代码片段 — 等宽字体 + 无衬线注释
// Type scale utility — switch families at runtime const setType = (serif, sans, mono) => { const root = document.documentElement; root.style.setProperty('--font-serif', serif); root.style.setProperty('--font-sans', sans); root.style.setProperty('--font-mono', mono); }; setType( 'Georgia, serif', "'Space Grotesk', sans-serif", "'Space Mono', monospace" );

等宽字体让代码对齐自然,注释和说明用无衬线保持清晰。好的 mono 字体在 14–16px 时仍然锐利,字符宽度一致让缩进层级一目了然。

用例 03 / Cards
卡片网格 — 无衬线 UI + 等宽标签
Typography
Scale & Rhythm
A modular scale keeps headings and body in proportion. Start with a base size and multiply by a consistent ratio.
Spacing
Vertical Baseline
Line-height should follow the body text, not the headings. A 1.5–1.75 range works for most serif body text.
Color
Contrast First
Body text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast. Muted grays are fine for metadata, but never for content readers scan.
Weight
Two Is Enough
Regular and bold cover 90% of needs. Adding a medium or semibold gives nuance without the weight-juggling.
